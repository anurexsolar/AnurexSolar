About us

Independent by choice. Obsessive about quality.

Anurex Solar was established in 2019, growing out of our parent business, Anura Hardware. Our main branch sits in Warakapola, with a second branch in Gampaha — keeping us close to every roof we work on.

Being an independent local business means no pre-packaged kits and no sales quotas. Every system starts from scratch: sized against your actual usage, roof orientation and budget, built from components we'd put on our own homes — then installed safely, neatly, and built to last.

And when you call, you reach the owner — D. P. A. K. Pathirana — not a call centre. That's how it's been since day one, and it's why our customers recommend us to their neighbours.
